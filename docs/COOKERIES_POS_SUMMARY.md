# Crockery & Cookeries POS — Feature Summary & Client Guide

**Omni POS Core — Industry Solution for Crockery, Kitchenware & Cookware Retail**

---

## 1. Executive Summary

The **Crockery & Cookeries POS** module is custom-built for retail businesses selling dinnerware, cookware, glassware, cutlery, kitchen organizers, and spare parts. It simplifies daily retail operations, eliminates stock discrepancies between complete sets and loose items, reduces breakage loss, and drives sales with automatic cross-selling.

---

## 2. Key Features & Business Value

### 🍽️ 1. Set vs. Loose Piece Selling ("Decanting")
* **The Challenge:** Customers frequently want to buy a replacement plate or bowl from an expensive 32-piece or 52-piece dinner set, or stores want to unbox a complete set into individual display pieces.
* **Our Solution:** A 1-click **"Decant Set to Pieces"** tool that instantly breaks down boxed sets into individual loose items, auto-calculates loose-piece pricing, and updates inventory counts accurately.

### 💔 2. Instant In-Store Breakage & Loss Tracking
* **The Challenge:** Fragile glass, opal, and ceramic products are prone to accidental drops, chipped rims, or transit damage.
* **Our Solution:** Cashiers can log breakage in 2 taps directly from the POS interface with damage reasons (*Customer Dropped*, *Chipped in Transit*, *Defective Glaze*) and audit notes, keeping inventory reconciled in real-time.

### 🎁 3. Gift & Protective Packaging Add-ons
* **The Challenge:** Cookware and tableware are premier gifts for weddings, housewarmings, and festive occasions.
* **Our Solution:** Add specialized packaging line items at checkout with one click:
  * **Bubble Wrap & Outer Box Protection** (+৳60)
  * **Luxury Gift Wrap with Satin Ribbon & Greeting Slip** (+৳150)
  * **Reinforced Wooden Transit Crate** (+৳350)
  * Capture recipient name and customized greeting messages printed directly on the sales receipt.

### ⚙️ 4. Cookware Warranty & Automatic Spare Parts Cross-Sell
* **The Challenge:** Pressure cookers and premium cookware require warranty tracking and recurring spare parts (gaskets, safety valves, handles) that cashiers often forget to suggest.
* **Our Solution:** 
  * Displays warranty period (e.g., 2–5 Year Warranty) clearly on product cards and receipts.
  * When a pressure cooker is added to the cart, the POS automatically pops up a **"Recommended Spares"** bar to cross-sell matching rubber gaskets, safety valves, and whistles with 1 tap.

### ⚡ 5. Smart Compatibility & Material Filters
* **Heat Compatibility Badges:** Instant visual chips for **Induction-Safe**, **Gas-Compatible**, **Microwave-Safe**, **Oven-Safe**, and **Dishwasher-Safe**.
* **Material Specifications:** Clear categorization by material (*Opal Glass, Fine Bone China, Ceramic, Stainless Steel 304, Cast Iron, Hard Anodized*).
* **Quick Filter Chips:** Allows cashiers to immediately filter the catalog when a customer asks: *"Show me only induction-ready cookware"* or *"Show me microwave-safe bowls"*.
