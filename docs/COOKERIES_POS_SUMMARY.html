📌 1. Executive Summary

The Crockery & Cookeries POS module is tailored specifically for stores selling tableware, cookware, glassware, cutlery, kitchen organizers, and pressure cookers. It addresses the unique daily retail friction in this industry: selling individual pieces out of complete dinner sets, logging fragile item drops, cross-selling matching cooker spare parts, and packaging luxury gift sets.

✨ 2. Core POS Capabilities

🍽️ Set vs. Loose Piece Decanting

Sell full 32-pc or 52-pc dinner sets, or break down a boxed set into individual loose plates and bowls with 1-click automatic stock conversion and calculated loose-item pricing.

💔 In-Store Breakage & Damage Log

Instantly record dropped, chipped, or damaged fragile items directly from the cashier screen with customizable damage audit reasons, keeping inventory 100% accurate.

🎁 Gift & Transit Packaging Add-ons

1-tap checkout add-on for bubble wrap protection (+৳60), luxury satin ribbon gift wrap (+৳150), or reinforced wooden crates (+৳350), with printed greeting messages.

⚙️ Cookware Spares Cross-Sell

When a pressure cooker or non-stick pan is added to the bill, the POS automatically suggests matching rubber gaskets, safety valves, and whistle weights.

⚡ Compatibility Badges & Filters

Fast filter chips for Induction-Safe, Microwave-Safe, and Warranty-Covered items to instantly answer customer questions on the floor.

🏷️ Material Specifications

Visual badges for materials: Opal Glass, Bone China, Ceramic, Melamine, Stainless Steel (304), Cast Iron, and Hard Anodized coatings.
